If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 999 (or your local emergency number) now.

If what you type into Companion suggests you may be in crisis, it shows a screen with helplines for where your phone is set: in the UK, 999 for emergencies, Samaritans (call 116 123), Shout (text SHOUT to 85258) and Childline (call 0800 1111, if you're under 19), and, where what you wrote mentions abuse, the National Domestic Abuse Helpline (call 0808 2000 247) and the NSPCC (call 0808 800 5000); in Ireland, 999 or 112, Samaritans Ireland (call 116 123), Pieta (call 1800 247 247), Text About It (text TALK to 50808) and Childline (call 1800 66 66 66), and, where abuse is mentioned, Women's Aid (call 1800 341 900); everywhere else, a reminder to call your local emergency number and a link to findahelpline.com, which lists free helplines in your country. These services are run by those organisations, not by us, and we can't guarantee their availability. No one at WordApp reads what you type into Companion.
