WordApp Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

WordApp ("we", "us", or "our") is a daily devotional app designed to support personal scripture study, reflection, and prayer. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights in relation to it.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the minimum information required to provide the service:

  • Account information: When you create an account, we collect your email address and a hashed password via Supabase Auth.
  • Devotional data: Your daily reflections, completed devotionals, reading streak, and chosen Bible translation are stored in our Supabase database and associated with your account.
  • Device information: Standard platform data (iOS/Android version, device type) used solely for crash reporting and compatibility.

We do not collect your name, phone number, location, contacts, camera access, or any biometric data.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app — storing your reflections and streak so they persist across devices.
  • To restore your progress if you reinstall the app or change devices.
  • To deliver premium features (if applicable) via RevenueCat subscription management.

We do not use your information for advertising, sell it to third parties, or share it with anyone outside the service providers listed below.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services, each operating under their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — Authentication and database storage. Data is stored in EU/US data centres with row-level security (you can only access your own data). Supabase Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat — In-app purchase and subscription management. Handles purchase receipts only; no financial data is stored by us. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, all associated data (reflections, streak, account credentials) is permanently deleted from our systems within 30 days. To request account deletion, contact us at the email below.

5. Children's Privacy

WordApp is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights including:

  • Access to the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion of your account and associated data
  • Data portability (export of your reflections)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after any change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
