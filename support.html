WordApp — Support

Help with the app · last updated 26 September 2026

WordApp is a daily devotional: one passage, one reflection, one prayer each morning. If something is not working, or you are unsure how a feature behaves, the answers below cover the questions we get most. Anything else — email us and a person replies.

Contact

Email: support@wordapp.app

Say which iPhone and iOS version you are on, which WordApp version (App Store → WordApp → Version History), and what you saw. We aim to reply within two working days.

Account

How do I delete my account?

Account controls live in Settings.

  1. Open WordApp and tap Settings at the bottom of the Home screen.
  2. Scroll to Data & Account and tap Delete account & data.
  3. Tap Delete on the confirmation.

Your account and every reflection synced to our servers are deleted immediately and permanently. Everything stored on the phone is cleared at the same time and you are signed out.

You do not need to contact us to delete your account — it is done from inside the app. If you cannot find the option, email us and we will delete it for you.

Do I need an account?

No. Tap Skip for now on the sign-in screen and your progress stays on your phone. Signing in adds cloud backup so your streak and reflections survive a new phone — you can sign in later from the completed-day screen (Sign in to sync your progress across devices).

Translations

Which Bible translations are available?

Tap the translation badge at the top of the day's passage to choose. King James Version (KJV), New International Version (NIV) and the Free Bible Version (FBV) are fetched live from the Bible service.

Why does a passage show a different translation than the one I picked?

The English Standard Version (ESV) and New King James Version (NKJV) are copyrighted texts that are not on our current licence. Pick either and the app shows the devotional's built-in ESV text, labelled ESV. The app never puts one translation's name on another translation's words.

If you are offline or the Bible service is unreachable, the app falls back to the built-in text so the day never goes blank. Reconnect and reopen the day to fetch your chosen translation.

The daily plan

How is each day's devotional chosen?

WordApp's devotional is a single, hand-written sequence — day one, day two, day three — and it is the same for every reader. Nothing is randomised and nothing is personalised: the passage you see on day five is the passage everyone sees on day five. Completing a day moves you to the next written day, so the plan always continues from where you left off, and the verse on the Home screen comes from the same written sequence. The day label at the top of each morning's devotional is your position in the plan.

Streaks

I missed a morning. Is my streak gone?

No. A missed morning never resets your streak. When you come back, WordApp tells you how many mornings you missed and asks, if you'd like to say, what got in the way; you can skip that and go straight to today's passage. Every reflection you wrote is kept.

What counts as completing a day?

Reading the passage and writing a reflection of at least a sentence (20 characters), then tapping Complete. One devotional per calendar day.

Reflections and export

How do I get my reflections out of the app?

On the completed-day screen, tap Export Reflection. The day's passage, reflection question, your response and the prayer are copied to the clipboard as Markdown — paste into Notes, Obsidian, or any journal. Your writing is yours.

Where are my past reflections?

Tap Reflection History on the completed-day screen to read every entry you have written.

Circles

Circle reading sessions

Open your Circle from the Circles tab, then tap into today's session to read the passage and answer the study prompts together. Your Circle's answers to a prompt reveal once you've posted your own answer to that same prompt, and you can edit your own answer any time — new answers appear live, so nobody has to leave and come back to see a friend's words.

How do I answer a prompt in a Circle session?
Open the session from your Circle, read the passage, then type your answer under a prompt and post it.

Why can't I see what my Circle wrote?
A prompt's answers reveal only after you've posted your own answer to that same prompt — post yours and theirs appear.

Can I change my answer after I've posted it?
Yes — tap your posted answer to edit it at any time.

Notes on a verse, shared with your Circle

While reading a passage in a Circle session, tap a verse and choose 'Add a note' to write something just about that verse. Only the people in your Circle can see it. Tap the verse again to read every note your Circle has left there.

How do I leave a note on a verse?
In a Circle session, tap the verse, choose Add a note, write it, then tap Add note.

Who can see the notes I leave on a verse?
Only the members of that Circle — verse notes are not visible outside your Circle.

Can I report or block someone over a note they left?
Yes — tap the note's author to block or report, the same safety tools as the rest of the Circle.

Circle Gatherings

Open the Gatherings tab inside a Circle to see any planned meet-up. Tap Yes, Maybe, or No to answer. If you lead the Circle, you can create a Gathering, share it, and confirm or cancel it once you have replies.

How do I RSVP to a Gathering?
Open the Circle's Gatherings tab and tap Yes, Maybe, or No on the card — change your answer any time before it's confirmed.

How do I plan a Gathering for my Circle?
As the Circle's leader, open the Gatherings tab and create one, then share it with your Circle.

Can I get a reminder for a Gathering?
Yes — a confirmed Gathering can sync a reminder to your phone so you don't miss it.

Can I find churches or food banks nearby?
In your Circle's Gatherings tab, under Find near you, tap Churches, Charity shops or Food banks. Apple Maps opens, searched for that near where you are.

Starting or joining a Circle

Open the Circles tab. To start one, tap 'Start a Circle', give it a name, the name you'll use in it, how you meet and a plan, then create it; you lead it. To join one, tap 'I have a code', enter the invite code someone shared with you and your name, then tap Join. A leader shares the invite from inside the Circle with 'Share the invite', 'Copy code' or the QR code.

How do I start a Circle?
Circles tab → Start a Circle → give it a name, your name in the Circle, how you meet and a plan.

Someone sent me a code. How do I join?
Circles tab → I have a code → enter the invite code and your name → Join. Codes expire after a while; if yours doesn't work, ask the leader for a fresh one.

How do I invite people to my Circle?
Open the Circle and use Share the invite, Copy code, or let them scan the QR code.

Prayer requests in your Circle

Open your Circle and tap the Prayer tab. Type into 'A request for the Circle' and tap Add. Members tap 'I prayed' when they have. When it's answered, the person who asked taps 'answered' and can add how. Tick 'Only the leaders can see this' for something you'd rather keep between you and the Circle's leaders.

How do I share a prayer request?
Open your Circle → Prayer tab → type your request → Add.

Who can see what I share in a Circle?
Only the members of that Circle. A request marked 'Only the leaders can see this' is seen by the Circle's leaders only. Nothing you share in a Circle is shown to other WordApp users.

Custom study plans for your Circle

As the Circle's leader, open the Sessions tab and scroll to Study Plans, then tap '+ Create a plan'. Give it a title, an optional description, and a pace, then tap Create. Tap 'Edit steps' to add passages (and an optional reflection question) one at a time, and 'Activate' to make it the Circle's current plan — 'Open next step' then opens the next unopened step as a session.

How do I create a study plan for my Circle?
As the leader, open the Sessions tab, scroll to Study Plans, tap '+ Create a plan', give it a title, then tap Create.

How do I add passages to a study plan?
Tap 'Edit steps' on the plan, then type a passage — e.g. John 3, or John 3:16-21 — and tap Add step. Repeat for each step.

How do I make my Circle start a plan I built?
Tap Activate on the plan. It becomes the Circle's active plan, and 'Open next step' opens its steps as sessions in order.

Leaving a Circle, and reporting or blocking a member

Open your Circle, tap a member's name to block or report them, or tap 'Leave this Circle' at the bottom to leave. Blocking hides that person's words from you; reporting tells us what happened so we can look into it. Leaving is immediate and can't be undone from inside the app — ask to be re-invited if you change your mind.

How do I leave a Circle?
Open the Circle, scroll to the bottom, and tap Leave this Circle.

How do I report someone in my Circle?
Tap their name, choose Report, describe what happened, and we'll look into it.

What does blocking someone in a Circle do?
It hides their words from you everywhere in that Circle, without removing them or telling them you blocked them.

Reading and listening

Learn — Bible Figures and Faith & Reason

On Home, under 'Go deeper', tap Learn to open a list of study shelves — Bible Figures and Faith & Reason today, with more added the same way over time. Tap a shelf to open it; the back arrow (or a swipe back) returns you to Learn.

Where did Bible Figures go?
It moved to Learn — on Home, tap Learn under 'Go deeper', then tap Bible Figures.

Where did Faith & Reason go?
It moved to Learn — on Home, tap Learn under 'Go deeper', then tap Faith & Reason.

What is the Learn hub?
One place for every study shelf — Bible Figures, Faith & Reason, and more as they're added — instead of each one having its own row on Home.

Text size

On Home, tap Settings at the bottom, go to Reading, and pick Small, Default, Large, or Largest under Text size. It applies to the devotional, the Bible, and Circle sessions.

How do I make the text bigger?
Settings → Reading → Text size — choose Large or Largest.

Where do I change the font size?
Settings → Reading → Text size.

Does text size affect the whole app?
It applies everywhere you read: the devotional, the Bible, and Circle sessions.

Listen to today's devotional

On the Home screen, under today's devotional, tap 'Listen' to have the passage and devotional read aloud; tap it again to stop. If you don't see it, turn it on in Settings → Reading → 'Read aloud control on the devotional'.

Can WordApp read the devotional out loud to me?
Yes — tap Listen under today's devotional on Home. Turn it on first in Settings → Reading if it's not showing.

Where is the listen button?
Under the passage on Home, once 'Read aloud control' is switched on in Settings → Reading.

How do I stop the reading?
Tap the same button again — it changes to Stop while it's speaking.

Siri, widget and reminders

Ask Siri for today's verse

Say 'Hey Siri, get today's verse from WordApp' and Siri speaks the verse of the day without opening the app. You can also add it as a Shortcut from the Shortcuts app for a faster way to trigger it.

Can I ask Siri for the verse of the day?
Yes — say 'Hey Siri, get today's verse from WordApp'.

How do I set up the WordApp Siri shortcut?
Open the Shortcuts app, add the 'Get Today's Verse' action from WordApp, and record a phrase if you want a custom one.

Morning reminder

On Home, tap Settings at the bottom, go to Reminders, turn on 'Daily reminder', and pick the hour. Reminders are local to your phone — nothing about your reading is sent anywhere. If it doesn't fire, check iPhone Settings → WordApp → Notifications → Allow Notifications.

How do I set a morning reminder?
Settings → Reminders → turn on Daily reminder and pick an hour.

My reminder isn't firing — what do I check?
iPhone Settings → WordApp → Notifications → Allow Notifications, then check the hour under Settings → Reminders in WordApp.

Where did the reminder setting move to?
It's in WordApp's Settings now, under Reminders — it is no longer on the completed-day screen.

Your account

Forgot your password

On the sign-in screen, tap 'Forgot your password?' under the password box, enter the email you signed up with and tap Send Reset Link. Open the email on this phone and tap Reset Password: WordApp opens on 'Choose a new password'. Save it and you're back in, straight to today's reading. The link works once and expires; if it has, tap 'Forgot your password?' again for a new one.

I forgot my password — how do I get back in?
On the sign-in screen tap 'Forgot your password?', enter your email, then tap the link in the email we send and choose a new password.

I didn't get the reset email.
Wait a minute or two and check your spam or junk folder. Make sure it's the email you signed up with; for privacy, the app shows the same message either way.

The reset link says it has expired.
Each link works once and for a limited time. Tap 'Forgot your password?' again for a fresh one.

Something is not working

  • The passage does not load: check your connection; the built-in text shows while offline. Force-quit and reopen the app.
  • The widget shows 0 or yesterday's verse: open the app once to push fresh data to the widget. If it persists after completing today's devotional, remove and re-add the widget.
  • The app crashes or freezes: update to the latest version in the App Store, then reopen. If it persists, email us with your iOS and WordApp versions.
  • Sign-in fails: check the email address; the confirmation email can land in spam. Forgot your password? Tap Forgot your password? on the sign-in screen. Skip for now still lets you read today.
  • I can't find my Circle: confirm you entered the invite code exactly as shared. Codes are case-sensitive. Ask your Circle leader to resend it.
